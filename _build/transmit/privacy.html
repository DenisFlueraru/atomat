The short version

Transmit OSC sends your phone’s sensor data only to the computer you choose (by typing its address or scanning a workshop code). We don’t run servers that receive your data, we don’t have accounts, and we don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What the app does with each kind of data

Motion, compass, air pressure, gestures, gamepad, faders, pads, touch Turned into OSC messages and sent over your local network to the receiver you entered. Nothing is stored.
Microphone
(only when audio is on)		 Analysed on the phone into numbers (loudness, pitch, onsets, frequency bands). No sound is recorded, stored or sent.
Camera
(only when camera, hands or the QR scanner is on)		 Analysed on the phone into numbers (face angles, body and hand points, average colour) or used to read a QR code. No images or video are recorded, stored or sent.
Location
(only when location is on)		 Sent as OSC to your receiver. Nothing is stored.
Location for temperature
(only when temperature → outside is on)		 Your position is rounded to about 1 km and sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo to get the current outside temperature and humidity. Open-Meteo’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that request.
Your name in the app Used only inside the OSC addresses sent to your receiver, and stored on your phone.
Settings, scenes, labels Stored on your phone only.

Messages sent back from your computer (labels, text, colours, vibration, flashlight) are received over your local network and are not stored.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; we only learn whether the unlock was bought, so the app can remove the free-version pauses.

Permissions

The app asks for camera, microphone, location and local-network access only when you switch on a feature that needs them. You can switch any of them off at any time in the app or in your phone’s settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don’t receive or store personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about this, or think something here is wrong, write to us and we’ll answer.
