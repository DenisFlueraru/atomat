Transmit OSC

How to use

OverviewHow to usePrivacyTerms
10 Hz1001 k17 kHz

1. Before you start

  • Your phone and your computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest and public networks often block devices from talking to each other — a phone hotspot or your own router works best.
  • On the computer, open something that receives OSC: Max/MSP, TouchDesigner, Pure Data, Ableton (Max for Live), Processing, Unity, Resolume… The app sends to port 9000 by default.
  • Find the computer’s IP address: on a Mac, System Settings → Wi-Fi → Details; on Windows, Settings → Network → Wi-Fi → Properties. It looks like 192.168.1.20.

2. Connect (link tab)

  1. Type your name. Every message starts with it — /anna/motion/tilt — so many phones never get mixed up. Spaces and accents are cleaned up automatically.
  2. Enter the receiver: the computer’s IP address and port (9000). Or tap scan workshop code and scan the QR code your workshop leader shows.
  3. Tap transmit. The button turns orange and shows how many messages per second are going out.

You can add up to three extra receivers in settings (top-right icon), to send to several computers at once.

3. Choose what to send (sense tab)

Every sensor has its own on/off button, and inside each sensor you can switch single values on or off. Only what is on gets sent. Camera, microphone and location ask for permission the first time you switch them on.

  • Motion — acceleration, gyroscope, tilt, shake energy
  • Compass and air pressure
  • Audio — loudness, peak, pitch, onsets (claps), 8 frequency bands. No sound leaves the phone.
  • Location — position, speed, accuracy; temperature outside and humidity
  • Gestures — shake, phone face up / face down
  • Camera — face (head angles, smile, eyes), body (13 points), average colour
  • Hands — palm, fingertips, pinch, grab
  • Gamepad — any Bluetooth game controller
  • Light, proximity, battery, Wi-Fi

Use zero on tilt and compass to make the current position the centre. Smoothing calms jittery values.

4. Play

  • Faders — 16 faders on two pages, values 0–1.
  • Pads — 16 pads. Each pad can be a hit (on while pressed) or a toggle. Tap the pencil to rename them.
  • XY — a multitouch pad, one point per finger.
  • Mix — faders and pads together, in the layout your workshop leader sets.
  • Keys and sequencer — play notes, or let the step sequencer generate patterns (full version: as MIDI).

Lock (the padlock on every tab) stops accidental touches while the sensors keep sending. Hold for about a second to unlock. Performer mode turns the screen black with a pulsing beat; tap five times quickly to leave.

5. Receive it on the computer

Max/MSP: [udpreceive 9000] → [route /anna] → [route /motion] … A ready-made receiving patch is available on request.

TouchDesigner: add an OSC In CHOP (numbers) or OSC In DAT (everything), set the port to 9000.

Anything else: listen for OSC over UDP on port 9000.

Addresses the phone sends

Every address starts with /yourname.

/motion/accel · /gyro · /tilt · /energymovement
/compass · /env/pressureheading, air pressure
/audio/rms · /peak · /pitch · /onset · /bandssound analysis (bands = 8 values)
/geo · /geo/speed · /geo/accuracylocation
/outside · /humidityweather where you are
/gesture/shake · /gesture/facegestures
/face/… · /body/… · /hands/… · /colorcamera tracking
/fader/1–16 · /pad/1–16 · /xy/ncontrols
/gamepad/… · /light · /proximity · /battery · /wifieverything else
/testsent when you tap “test” in settings

6. The computer can talk back

The phone listens on port 9001 (you can change it to 9002 or 9003 in settings). Send to /yourname/… for one phone or /all/… for every phone:

msg "text"show a message on the screen (no text clears it)
label/fader/n "text" · label/pad/n "text"rename a fader or pad
fader/n 0–1 · pad/n 0|1move a fader, light a pad
color r g b [a]tint the screen (values 0–1, no values clears)
vibrate · flash 0|1vibrate, flashlight on/off
sensor/motion 0|1 …switch a sensor on or off
lock 0|1 · scene "name"lock the screen, load a saved scene
bpm n · beat n · performer 0|1tempo, beat and performer mode

7. MIDI (full version)

On iPhone, MIDI goes out over the network MIDI session and to any connected MIDI device; on Android over USB or Bluetooth MIDI. Faders send CC 1–16. The sequencer can follow Ableton’s MIDI clock.

8. Free and full version

Everything works in the free version, except MIDI out, and sending pauses for 10 seconds every 30 seconds. The one-time full version removes the pauses and adds MIDI. Bought it before? Use already bought? at the bottom of settings.

9. If nothing arrives

  • Check both devices are on the same network, and the IP and port are right.
  • Allow incoming connections for Max / TouchDesigner in the computer’s firewall (macOS asks the first time).
  • On iPhone, allow Local Network for Transmit OSC: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Try a phone hotspot — some routers isolate devices from each other.
  • Tap test in settings: if /yourname/test arrives, the connection works.

Still stuck? Write to contact@atomatlab.com with your phone model and what you’re sending to — we’ll help.
