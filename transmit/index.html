Transmit OSC

Turn every phone in the room into an instrument. Motion, sound, face, body and hand tracking, faders and pads — sent as OSC over Wi-Fi to Max/MSP, TouchDesigner, Pure Data, Ableton or anything that speaks OSC.

Made by atomat lab for workshops, performances and installations. Everyone types a name, scans a code, and every phone gets its own addresses — /anna/motion/tilt — so twelve phones never get mixed up.

What it sends

MotionAcceleration, gyroscope, tilt, shake energy

Built for workshops

  • Scan a QR code to connect in seconds
  • Switch every sensor, and every part of it, on or off
  • The computer talks back: rename faders, send messages, colour the screen, vibrate, flash
  • Lock the screen — sensors keep sending
  • Scenes, smoothing, record and loop, several receivers
  • MIDI out over the network, USB or Bluetooth (full version)
