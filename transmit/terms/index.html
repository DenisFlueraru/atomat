1. The app

Transmit OSC sends your phone’s sensors, camera tracking and on-screen controls as OSC (and, in the full version, MIDI) to a computer or device you choose, usually on your own local network. It is a tool for workshops, performances, installations and experiments.

3. Free version and full version

  • The app is free to download. In the free version sending pauses for 10 seconds every 30 seconds, and MIDI out is off.
  • The full version is a one-time in-app purchase (shown in the app in your local currency, €4.99 in the EU). It removes the pauses and turns on MIDI out on that store account, for good. There is no subscription.

5. Using it safely

  • Don’t use the app while driving, cycling or anywhere you need your full attention.
  • The computer you connect to can make your phone vibrate, turn on the flashlight and change the screen colour. Only connect to computers you trust, and be careful with the flashlight near people’s eyes.

  • OSC on a local network is not encrypted. Don’t use the app to send anything private.
