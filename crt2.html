Submersible contact microphone

CRT2

CRT2 is a rugged, multi-purpose contact microphone designed for surface and underwater sound exploration. A refined version of the CRT1.

Sink it in creeks, aquariums and rain barrels as a compact alternative to a hydrophone, or strap it to pipes, trees, machines and instruments with the velcro strap and textured mounting base. The copper shielding and internal capacitor tame harsh upper frequencies for a smoother, more musical tone — a rugged contact microphone for field recording on land and underwater.
