48V phantom-powered contact microphone

FIELD5

FIELD5 is a 48V phantom-powered contact microphone designed to listen in any circumstance. With the included accessories it fits most structures and objects.

Use it for field recording, sound design and experimental music: attach it to metal, wood, glass or ice with the included magnet, suction cup or velcro mounts, submerge it for underwater recording, and capture the vibrations travelling inside materials directly into any recorder or audio interface with 48V phantom power. Epoxy-sealed and copper-shielded, with a male Neutrik XLR, it is built for both field and studio work — no external preamp required.
