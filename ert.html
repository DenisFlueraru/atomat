ERT

ERT is an electromagnetic field microphone that reveals the hidden soundscape of electronic devices — clocks, screens, chargers and invisible ambient hum.

Sweep it over laptops, phone screens, chargers, routers, LED signs and motors to record glitchy tones, data-like pulses and hidden electrical hums. An EMF microphone (inductive coil pickup) is a staple tool for sound designers, electroacoustic composers and sonic explorers — plug and play with any recorder or audio interface via 6.3 mm jack.
